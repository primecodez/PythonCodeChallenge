Python's operator precedence is combining the 
classic math acronym PEMDASC with the logic acronym NAO.

Parentheses (): Always evaluated first.

Exponents **: Evaluated second.

Multiplication * & Division /, //, %: Evaluated left-to-right.

Addition + & Subtraction -: Evaluated left-to-right.

Comparisons ==, >, <: Evaluated after all math is complete.

N.A.O. (Not, And, Or): Boolean logic evaluates exactly in this order.

Assignment =: Always evaluates absolutely last, moving right-to-left.
